HAVEN

IN CYBERSPACE AS IT IS IN HEAVEN

HAVEN DID NOT BEGIN AS A COMPANY OR A PLATFORM. IT BEGAN AS A THOUGHT THAT WOULD NOT LEAVE.

FOR MOST OF HUMAN HISTORY, THE PLACES WHERE PEOPLE SOUGHT TRUTH WERE BUILT OF STONE AND TIMBER. CATHEDRALS. TEMPLES. MONASTERIES. QUIET ROOMS WHERE A PERSON COULD SIT WITH THEIR THOUGHTS LONG ENOUGH FOR SOMETHING REAL TO APPEAR.

BUT THE CENTER OF LIFE HAS DRIFTED ELSEWHERE NOW. MUCH OF IT UNFOLDS INSIDE A NEW TERRITORY THAT HAS NO SOIL AND NO SKY. WE CALL IT CYBERSPACE. IT MOVES QUICKLY, SPEAKS LOUDLY, AND REWARDS WHATEVER CAPTURES ATTENTION THE FASTEST.

MEANING RARELY SURVIVES LONG IN SUCH A PLACE.

HAVEN IS AN ATTEMPT TO BUILD SOMETHING DIFFERENT THERE.

NOT A NETWORK FOR NOISE. NOT ANOTHER SYSTEM DESIGNED TO CAPTURE AND HOLD ATTENTION. HAVEN IS AN ATTEMPT TO BUILD A QUIET STRUCTURE IN THE DIGITAL WORLD—A PLACE WHERE SIGNAL CAN REMAIN INTACT, WHERE THOUGHT CAN SLOW DOWN, AND WHERE HUMAN PRESENCE IS NOT REDUCED TO AN ALGORITHM.

IN OLDER CENTURIES PEOPLE BUILT CATHEDRALS TO HOLD LIGHT AND SILENCE. HAVEN ASKS A SIMPLER QUESTION FOR OUR OWN TIME.

WHAT WOULD A CATHEDRAL OF THOUGHT LOOK LIKE IN CYBERSPACE?

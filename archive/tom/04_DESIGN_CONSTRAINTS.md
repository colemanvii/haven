# Design Constraints

These constraints are non-negotiable.

## Materials
- Warm, bone-matte polymer or equivalent
- No glossy finishes
- No exposed glass surfaces intended for touch

## Light
- Warm amber only
- No blue light
- No hard on/off states
- Light must rise and fall gradually

## Input
- Physical rotary wheel
- Weighted rotation with resistance
- No touchscreens as primary input

## Display
- Single line of text
- High contrast
- Low density
- Legible in peripheral vision

## Behavior
- Intentional latency in all interactions
- No animations designed to excite
- No notifications without explicit user action

## Form
- Handheld
- Pocketable
- Designed to disappear when not in use

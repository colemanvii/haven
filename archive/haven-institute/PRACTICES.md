# PRACTICES — Creative Physics

These practices are simple.
They are meant to be used, not admired.

## Daily Practice

### 1. Settle the System
Slow breath. Feel the body. Let urgency drain.

### 2. Check the Three Systems
- Feeling — What do I care about right now?
- Meaning — What does this moment ask of me?
- Motion — What is the smallest true next act?

### 3. One Clean Act
Do one thing. Stop before depletion.

## Weekly Practice

Review where wattage increased, where it leaked, where you rushed, and where you tended well.

Re-center on signal.

Adjust pace, not ambition.

## The Stop Rule

When you notice irritability, performative thinking, urgency without clarity, or loss of care: stop.

## Closing Note

This is not optimization.
It is stewardship.
